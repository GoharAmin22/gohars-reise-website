Partnership model

For international brands, partnerships can be developed across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube for the German-speaking market, only after Holger has tested the product or service and confirmed an authentic editorial fit.

Paid social pilot

One agreed Instagram Reel or TikTok integration built around a genuine personal or everyday story — not a generic ad read.

Longer-term partnership

If the pilot performs and the product remains a good fit, recurring work or a performance component can be discussed.

Rights stay explicit

Paid media, Spark Ads/whitelisting, raw footage, translations, editing rights, exclusivity and extended usage are negotiated separately.

Editorial standard: no paid religious rulings, no claims of scholarly authority, no unverified religious or health claims, and transparent advertising disclosure.
